From Nepal. With another purpose.

We work with recovered materials and purchased fabric naturally dyed by a collaborator.

Local hands. Considered work.

Our pieces are made in Nepal through close collaboration with local women. Together, we upcycle discarded materials into bags and pouches for everyday use.

Naturally dyed canvas & cotton.

Alongside recovered materials, we use canvas and cotton fabric that we purchase and have naturally dyed by a collaborator. These fabrics bring colour and texture to our bags and pouches. Shades can vary between dye batches, giving the fabric its own character.

Made possible by what you share.

Thank you to everyone who donates fabric and discarded materials to TAMANG Nepal. Your contributions give us materials to work with and help keep them in use. Together with our makers and collaborators, you are part of each new beginning.

Thank you to NNMGA, @malla.mountaineer and @dk__adrenalinevertex for donating materials.

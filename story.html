A different way of looking

What if waste
was a resource?

A useful next chapter for something that was being thrown away.

TAMANG grew from three friends’ frustration at the waste around them in Nepal. They wanted to give discarded materials a useful purpose and build a practice connected to local people.

The idea was to make objects people would want to use: useful in their form, connected to their origin, and part of ordinary life.

The person behind a beginning

A concern
that stayed.

Born and raised in Kakani, Yujin Tamang, also known as Sita, became aware of plastic pollution while still at school.

Later, through work with an NGO, a volunteer from the Netherlands introduced her to crocheting with plastic. That encounter offered a way to act on a concern she had carried for years.

In 2023, she turned the idea into a small business, bringing material reuse and local making together.

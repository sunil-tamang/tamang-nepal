From Nepal.
With another purpose.

We work with recovered materials and
purchased fabric naturally dyed by
a collaborator.

Local hands. Considered work.

Our pieces are made in Nepal through close collaboration with skilled makers. Together we explore how recovered materials and naturally dyed fabrics can take on a new life.

Colour through collaboration.

We work with a collaborator to develop natural dyes, creating considered colour from local knowledge and natural sources.

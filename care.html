01 / Cleaning

Start gently.

A little attention before a bigger clean.

For a small mark, begin with a soft damp cloth. Avoid harsh cleaning products and high heat. Check the confirmed care of your particular piece before washing it.

The fabric, dye, lining and recovered components can need different care. A product’s appearance alone does not tell you how it should be cleaned.

Ask about your piece ↗

02 / Storage

A good place
between uses.

Let the confirmed materials guide the routine.

Keep the piece away from prolonged direct sunlight and high heat. Allow it to dry before putting it away, following the care instructions confirmed for its materials.

When you ask about care, include the product name and the version you have. Construction and materials can vary between pieces.

03 / Naturally dyed fabric

Know your cloth.

Colour and botanical marks can vary. Ask about fibre, dye and tested care for the available piece rather than assuming a standard washing method.

trying a method.

A short description helps us understand the piece.

Tell us the product name and what you are concerned about: a mark, colour change, seam or closure. Ask for guidance before using a cleaning product or planning a repair.

A local beginning

A concern.
A way to act.

Yujin Tamang grew up in Kakani. Her concern about plastic pollution began at school; an introduction to plastic crochet later offered a practical way to respond.

That idea became part of TAMANG: connecting the materials around us with skills that can be shared close to home.

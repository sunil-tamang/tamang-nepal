“What has served one purpose
can find another.”

TAMANG began with three friends in Kakani, Nepal, who saw potential in materials that others had finished using. We make useful products from recovered materials and naturally dyed fabrics, with the skills of local women.

People, materials and place

Useful work.
Shared possibilities.

A different way of looking at what’s around us.

Preparing, crocheting, cutting and stitching create a reason to learn and make together. Our work connects local skills with materials that can still be useful.

It began with a concern about waste, and with a place to call home.

## Begin with what you can see

Colour, shape and texture are a first introduction. They cannot tell you everything about a lining, structural layer or closure. Understanding a piece means looking beyond its outer surface.

## Different components, different jobs

The Toiletries Bag brings naturally dyed outer fabric together with recovered tent lining and hand-crocheted plastic within the structure. Those components contribute different things to the finished pouch.

## Fit before choosing

Ask for the internal measurements of the available piece and compare them with what you want to carry. For a Laptop Cover, device dimensions, opening and padding all deserve a specific answer.

## Continue with care

The cloth, dye and recovered components may need different attention. Ask about confirmed care rather than assuming that every bag can be washed in the same way. A useful object deserves a routine suited to its materials.

## Start with the useful parts

A recovered rice sack has already carried something. Its weave and condition help determine which areas can have another use. Preparation begins with reading the available material.

## A weave with a history

The original texture is part of what makes the material recognisable. Reworking a sack changes its form while retaining a connection to its earlier purpose.

## Follow the available material

TAMANG makes different tote variants, including versions using recovered rice sacks. The construction and material source can vary, so ask which version is available rather than assuming every tote is the same.

## Choose for the everyday

Dimensions, strap drop and lining matter when choosing a bag. Understanding how a piece will be used helps connect material reuse with something practical.

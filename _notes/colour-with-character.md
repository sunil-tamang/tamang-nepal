## A different origin

TAMANG purchases fabric and works with a collaborator who dyes it using natural materials. The cloth is not made from the recovered plastic. Its own origin matters, even when it becomes part of a piece with several materials.

## Marks in the cloth

Natural colour and botanical marks give the fabric individual character. The exact fibre, pattern and colour are confirmed for the available piece. The cream Tote Bag and pink Toiletries Bag show different expressions of this fabric story.

## From cloth to form

Local women cut and stitch the cloth into finished pieces. An outer fabric, lining and structural layer can each do a different job, so the fabric you see is only one part of the construction.

## Keep the care specific

Before washing, ask about the fibre, dye and confirmed care of your particular piece. A soft damp cloth is a gentle starting point for a small mark. Avoid harsh products and high heat.

## A shared practice

The finished object is one part of the work. Preparing materials, hand-crocheting, sewing and finishing create opportunities to develop skills through practical tasks.

## Close to home

TAMANG works with women in the community around Kakani. Making locally connects the practice to the place where the idea began and the people who contribute to it.

## Different tasks, shared attention

Each stage asks for care. Plastic needs to be prepared before crocheting; cloth needs to be read before cutting. Assembling the parts brings those different kinds of attention into one form.

## Room to keep learning

Learning is part of a practice that continues through the work itself. A useful piece is a reason to share a technique, look again at a material and keep developing what can be made.

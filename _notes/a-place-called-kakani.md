## Where the idea took root

Kakani, in Nuwakot, Nepal, is home to TAMANG’s beginnings. The local community is part of the reason the practice has taken this form: materials, learning and making close to home.

## A concern that stayed

Yujin Tamang, also known as Sita, was born and raised in Kakani. Her awareness of plastic pollution began at school. Later, through work with an NGO, a volunteer from the Netherlands introduced her to plastic crochet.

## An idea becomes work

In 2023, Yujin turned the idea into a small business. TAMANG began with three friends who wanted to give discarded materials a useful purpose and connect that work to local people.

## A place in the everyday

The label’s story lives in the preparation, handwork and finishing as much as in the objects themselves. Every piece carries a connection to the place where the idea began.

## Illustrative placeholders

`editorial/polythene.webp`, `editorial/rice-sacks.webp`, `editorial/recovered-textiles.webp`, `editorial/natural-dye.webp`, `objects/kakani.webp` and `scenes/tote-right.webp` are generated illustrative images from the earlier design work. They are not documentary evidence, exact product records or named maker portraits. The site identifies illustrative imagery through alternative text, captions and a footer note. Replace them with verified brand photos using `IMAGE-REPLACEMENT.md`.

The hero scene is illustrative. Product detail galleries, enquiry records, saved items and comparison links use the actual product photos. No screenshots are used as website pages.

## Instagram

Four real selected posts have local thumbnails in `assets/images/instagram/`; exact post permalinks and captions are in `_data/instagram.yml`. They come from the official website's public Instagram cache and link to @tamang_nepal originals. This is a curated selection, not a live API feed. The optional native `https://www.instagram.com/embed.js` loads only after View here is clicked. Local thumbnails and original links remain if embedding is blocked.

## Logo and typography

`assets/images/tamang-logo.svg` is the supplied thin TAMANG wordmark with mountain icon only, with no extra tagline. The favicon uses its mountain mark.

Headings use self-hosted Cormorant Garamond at regular weight. Body text uses the existing self-hosted Inter. Both are distributed with their SIL Open Font Licenses in `assets/fonts`. Cormorant Garamond source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cormorantgaramond. No external font service or license subscription is needed.

Use TAMANG photographs and identity for the user's own TAMANG project; no broader image-rights grant is implied.

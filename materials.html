The materials we work with.

Recovered materials and naturally dyed fabrics have different beginnings.

From mountain shelters to everyday objects.

Tents collected from the mountains of Nepal are cleaned, sorted and upcycled into TAMANG Nepal products. Fabric once used for shelter finds a new purpose in everyday bags and pouches.

  1. 01RecoverTents are collected and cleaned.
  2. 02ConsiderEach piece is assessed for character.
  3. 03ReworkFabric is cut and prepared by hand.
  4. 04ReimagineTransformed into lasting objects.
